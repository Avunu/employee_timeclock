# Glyphs

`time.svg` and `timer.svg` are 32px icons from IBM's [Carbon Design System](https://github.com/carbon-design-system/carbon)
(`@carbon/icons` 11.88.0), used unmodified, under the Apache License 2.0.

`tools/build_kiosk_icons.py` sets them white on a coloured tile to make the kiosk pages' app icons in
`employee_timeclock/public/images/kiosk/`.
